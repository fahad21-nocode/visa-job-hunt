# Sponsored job search: operating approach (from 26 Nov 2026)

Replaces the earlier wide sweep-and-send approach. Drafts only: nothing is ever sent by Claude. Fahad sends manually, staggered.

## 1. Fewer, better applications
- From each weekly sweep, shortlist the top 10-15 roles only.
- For each shortlisted role, find the hiring manager or recruiter. Order of tools: Hunter free allowance first, Monid only when stuck (check price first).
- Save a short follow-up email as a Gmail draft, to be sent within 2 days of Fahad applying. Any address that is inferred rather than found is labelled as such.
- Fahad approves every application before it goes in.

## 2. Warm contacts first
- Draft LinkedIn or email messages to existing contacts asking for a referral or introduction at a sponsor. Drafts only.
- No fabricated premises (no "as we discussed" unless Fahad confirms it happened).

## 3. Consultancy channel
- Weekly sweep of current UK roles at Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant, NTT DATA, Capgemini, Accenture and Big 4 managed services.
- Apply only where the honest-fit score passes the gate below.

## 4. Temporary Shortage List roles
- Roles at RQF 3-5 on the Temporary Shortage List are prioritised, with an apply gate of 3.5/5 (RQF 6+ stays at 4.0/5).
- The Certificate of Sponsorship must be assigned by 31 Dec 2026.
- TSL status is checked against the current gov.uk list for each role, never assumed.

## Every role goes through the four gates
Sponsor licence, SOC code and salary (new-entrant path first), honest profile fit, employer willingness. See the career-ops rules.

## Do-not-contact rule
29 outreach emails were sent on 26 Nov 2026 (list in `outreach/sent-2026-11-26.csv`).
- No follow-up drafts for them before 30 Nov 2026.
- No one on that list is contacted again unless they have replied. As of the 26 Nov check, all 29 threads had one message (no replies).
- Every new batch is checked against that file first.

## Daily output
`daily/YYYY-MM-DD-send-list.md`: what to send, to whom, in what order, with gaps of at least an hour between messages.
