## Full description

Role is internal IT (Enabling Functions), not client-facing, supporting Deloitte's own
new cloud-based HR solution (SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central) launching early 2027
for UK and Swiss businesses. Acts as SME/Design Authority for SuccessFactors EC
technical configuration, stakeholder engagement across product owners, and
incident/problem management using ServiceNow ITSM and Azure DevOps.

Essentials:
- SAP Certified Application Associate – SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Core
- Prior experience supporting Employee Central

In-depth understanding of several of: Employee/Employment Data Portlets, Position
Management, Foundation Objects/MDF Framework, Business Rules/Workflows/Event Reasons,
Role-Based Permissions (RBP), Time Off, Global Benefits.

Desirables: Degree or equivalent in Computer Science/Information Systems; additional
SuccessFactors certifications (Platform, Reporting, Integration Suite, Recruiting,
Compensation).
